# Limitations and Future Work

## Current limitations

1. Only six reconstructed well groups are available.
2. W04 contains only 99 rows and differs materially from the other groups.
3. NTG is a derived binary indicator with incomplete independent lineage.
4. K is a severe target proxy.
5. VSH and RT are essentially rank-equivalent, limiting the physical meaning of RT prediction.
6. A large fraction of rows repeat adjacent states, reducing effective independence.
7. The dataset lacks independent tool-selection outcomes, boundary geometry, measurement noise, costs, and consequences.
8. Results cannot establish transfer to other fields or formations.

## Future work

- Obtain independently defined pay labels.
- Validate on additional fields and time-separated wells.
- Add independent measured resistivity and tool-response outcomes.
- Use depth-block bootstrap or conformal intervals with more wells.
- Evaluate formal domain-shift detection.
- Couple the advisory classifier to physics-based electromagnetic modeling.
- Establish a cost-sensitive decision framework with accountable expert approval.
