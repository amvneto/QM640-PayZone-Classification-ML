# Research Questions, Hypotheses, and Final Determinations

| RQ | Research question | Working hypothesis | Final determination |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | Can well-log variables classify the available pay indicator on a complete well excluded from training? | Reduced models retain useful discrimination, but grouped performance is lower than random-split performance. | Partly supported: five useful held-out wells, but W04 failed. |
| RQ2 | How do validation strategy and feature lineage affect performance and calibration? | Random splitting and target-derived variables inflate apparent performance. | Supported: nonlinear random splits were optimistic and K nearly reproduced the target. |
| RQ3 | Which variables and thresholds remain stable across wells? | PHIT and VSH are more transferable than depth, zone, and derived variables. | Supported with qualification: directions and PHIT thresholds were stable, but W04 did not transfer. |
| RQ4 | How accurately can RT be reconstructed, and how does uncertainty affect tool screening? | Nonlinear regression improves simple baselines and uncertain cases should be escalated. | Numerical reconstruction was strong, but independent physical prediction and automatic tool selection were not validated. |
