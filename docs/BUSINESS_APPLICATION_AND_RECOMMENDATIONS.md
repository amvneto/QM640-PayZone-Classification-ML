# Business Application and Recommendations

## Recommended use

Use the selected model as an advisory baseline that presents:

- pay-indicator probability;
- probability-quality warning;
- held-out-well similarity or out-of-range flag;
- disagreement between reduced models;
- resistivity-lineage warning; and
- expert-escalation status.

## Mandatory escalation conditions

Escalate to petrophysical and electromagnetic review when any of the following occurs:

- probability lies near the operating threshold;
- calibration is poor for the relevant well type;
- the case is outside the training range;
- models disagree materially;
- target-proxy variables drive the result;
- the resistivity interval crosses a screening boundary; or
- no comparable held-out well achieved acceptable performance.

## Prohibited use

The current dataset must not be used by itself to:

- certify economic pay;
- make an automatic deep-versus-extra-deep service choice;
- direct a well trajectory;
- replace formation evaluation; or
- claim independent RT prediction.

## Additional data required for tool selection

Boundary geometry, independent measured resistivity, depth-of-detection outcomes, noise assumptions, expert tool-selection labels, and service-cost or consequence data.
