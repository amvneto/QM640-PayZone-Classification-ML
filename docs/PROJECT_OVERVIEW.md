# Project Overview

## Business problem

Highly laminated formations can change over short vertical and lateral distances. A model that appears accurate under random row-level validation may fail on a new well because adjacent samples are correlated and some interpreted variables may already encode the target.

## Purpose

The capstone evaluates a leakage-resistant, explainable workflow that:

- reproduces the available pay/non-pay indicator;
- tests complete wells excluded from training;
- audits feature origin and target-proxy risk;
- evaluates probability calibration and missingness sensitivity;
- reconstructs the supplied resistivity channel while testing circularity; and
- converts uncertainty into an advisory expert-escalation framework.

## Target users

Geosteering engineers, petrophysicists, logging-while-drilling planners, drilling engineers, and subsurface managers.

## Scope boundary

Included: tabular well-log analysis, grouped validation, calibration, explainability, uncertainty, and advisory screening.

Excluded: three-dimensional reservoir construction, electromagnetic inversion, real-time closed-loop drilling, reserves estimation, production forecasting, and automatic trajectory control.
